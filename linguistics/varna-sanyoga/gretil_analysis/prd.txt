## Data Sources

### GRETIL Classification Table (GCT)
**File**: `gretil_classification.tsv` (tab-separated)
- `Group`: Literary category (Samhita, Brahmana, Upanisad, Sutra, Kavya, Purana, etc.)
- `Work`: Text identifier matching GRETIL corpus
- `WorkUrl`: Direct URL to plaintext version

### GRETIL Sound Corpus (GSC)
**File**: `gretil_sounds.csv` (comma-separated)
- **Rows**: Sanskrit phonemes (a, ā, i, ī, u, ū, etc.)
- **Columns**: Text identifiers (matching GCT Work column)
- **Values**: Integer counts of phoneme occurrences per text
