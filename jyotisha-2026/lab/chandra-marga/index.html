A self-contained gallery for lunar crescent strip images generated by the chandra-marga event pipeline and rendered through Stellarium.

Reading Notes

  • Moon-altitude strips fix the Moon near +8 degrees and compare crescent visibility across selected tithis.
  • The Sun-altitude strip fixes twilight at Sun altitude -9 degrees for the Shukla pratipada series.
