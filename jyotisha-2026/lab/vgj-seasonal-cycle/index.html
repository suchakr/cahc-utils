Narration

Summary

  • Base 27-proxy fit reaches its lowest mean boundary error near -1200 with 0.35 degrees mean error.
  • The strongest base window, defined as within 1 degree of that minimum, spans -1550 to -900.
  • The nine seasonal representatives are most compact near -1200 with 0.00 degrees mean error.
  • The coarse 83-star family has its best sampled epoch at -1000; because the seed is sampled every 500 years, this is a broad qualitative check rather than a fine minimum.
  • Abhyankar-style substitutions move the scenario minimum to -1200 with 0.51 degrees mean error, so representative choice remains a visible sensitivity.
  • The solar month-cycle panel is staged from the vendored nakshatra-bound seed and is explicitly not an Astropy-regenerated transit calculation.
  • The source-file table lists 7 local seed files used for this Stage 1 reconstruction.
  • Qualitative validation: the reproduced chart family keeps the expected broad BCE minimum window and preserves the interpretability of the representative-star sensitivity comparisons.
